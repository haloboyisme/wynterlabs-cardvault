# CardVault V3.2 — Streamer preview, feedback, and pack recap

Planned V3.2 scope, updated September 8, 2026. Roadmap proposal only; implementation has not started. This introduces several related subsystems and will need a detailed implementation design before development.

## Requested experience

Make the existing scan preview more polished for ordinary users, YouTube creators, and streamers. Preserve the card details currently displayed. All decorative effects are optional, configurable per account, and removable so plain scanning remains available.

## Preview and effects

- Reveal choices directly in the preview screen: instant appearance/no animation, fade in, and card-back-to-front flip.
- Optional pack-opening presentation, visual accents, and a 3D-style card treatment. Full 3D rendering remains subject to performance evaluation; do not require it for the core preview.
- Preserve current card details by default; let the owner choose visible details, layout, size, and appearance. Verify actual existing fields before implementation.
- Enable/disable individual effects, change their settings, and remove active effects. Provide a plain preset and reset-to-default controls.
- Suggested presets: Plain, Subtle, and Streamer. Suggested controls: reveal duration, hold duration, card scale, background, accent color, and effect intensity.
- Respect reduced-motion settings. Presentation animations must not independently confirm a scan or command feeder movement.

## Sound feedback

- Configurable confirmation and rejection sounds, with optional special-pull and pack-complete sounds.
- Account page contains master mute, volume, and per-event sound choices/off switches. Preview includes a convenient temporary mute and test controls.
- Offer selectable built-in sound/effect choices. Uploading custom media is an optional extension to evaluate, not an assumed requirement for the first implementation.
- Distinguish rejected/unrecognized scans from confirmed cards; avoid celebrating an unconfirmed match. Sound playback must not affect scan success.
- Choose where audio plays: scanner page or OBS overlay, avoiding doubled sounds when both are open. Browser audio activation and OBS playback need explicit compatibility testing.

## Pop-out and OBS overlay

- Provide a clean pop-out preview and a copyable URL for an OBS Browser Source.
- Background choices: transparent alpha, green, blue, or a custom solid color. Transparent live overlays and transparent exported video are separate capabilities.
- Display card reveals, selected details, optional sounds, and recap playback without account navigation, camera controls, or private administrative information.
- Bind overlays to their owner's chosen scanning session. Use a dedicated revocable read-only overlay credential; possession permits viewing only the selected presentation, never feeder control or account access.
- Account owner can rotate/disable the URL. The URL must be treated as a private viewing link.
- Reconnecting or refreshing must not replay old pull celebrations or duplicate audio automatically. Provide an explicit replay control.
- OBS must be able to reach the CardVault host; an overlay URL does not itself make a private LAN site publicly accessible.

## Finish Pack and saved video

- Interpret the requested final scan as an explicit Finish Pack/Finish Session action after the last physical card. This avoids requiring a special extra card scan.
- Freeze a recap of confirmed cards, respecting corrections and excluding rejected attempts. Intentional duplicate cards remain separate pulls.
- Suggested default: animated pack opening, cards revealed in scan order with their details, selected highlights, and a final spread of every card plus a count.
- Alternative styles: quick card fan, individual flips, or a plain summary grid. The user chooses all-cards versus highlights presentation; an all-cards recap remains available.
- Allow replay and saving a video without rescanning or operating the feeder. Include download/export in the V3.2 target, alongside recording playback in OBS.
- Design must settle supported export format, audio inclusion, duration, aspect ratio, rendering location, and browser support. Do not promise alpha video export solely because the live overlay is transparent.
- Suggested output layouts: landscape for YouTube/OBS and portrait for short videos. Use original generic pack graphics rather than assuming permission to reproduce branded packaging.

## Delivery and validation boundaries

Separate implementation design into account preferences, preview rendering/audio, session overlay transport, and recap/video export. Verify ownership isolation, plain mode, mute persistence, detail preservation, reconnect behavior, corrected scans, duplicate cards, OBS transparency/audio, reduced motion, and actual exported-video playback.

Wi-Fi feeders remain account-owned as previously discussed. Bluetooth support remains deferred to V3.5, targeting iPhone, Android, and computers subject to platform compatibility. Neither roadmap entry starts implementation.

## Reference

OBS Browser Source supports webpage URLs, visual/audio content, and transparent backgrounds: https://obsproject.com/kb/browser-source . This establishes a suitable integration mechanism, not proof of CardVault compatibility or implementation.
